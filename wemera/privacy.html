wemera Privacy Policy

Effective date / 시행일 / 施行日: 2026-11-07

Your photos stay on your device

wemera is a camera app. All photos you capture are processed and stored only on your device. We do not upload, collect, or have any access to your photos. wemera has no server of its own and no user accounts.

What the app does not collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, location, or photos. There is no sign-up or login.

Two things leave the device: the crash and error diagnostics and the usage analytics described below. Neither contains any part of your photos or anything you typed.

Crash and error diagnostics

When the app crashes or a feature fails, we collect diagnostic information so we can find the cause and fix it. This includes the error and where it happened (stack trace), your device model, OS version, app version, a record of the app starting and stopping, and a random install number the app created.

It does not include your photos, anything you shot, text you typed, your name, email, phone number, or location. The app is set not to keep your IP address in error records, though it can still be passed along in the connection. It is not used to identify you, and it is never used for advertising or marketing.

  • Processed by: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry)
  • Transferred to: United States
  • Purpose: diagnosing and fixing app errors
  • Retention: up to 90 days, then deleted
  • How to stop it: deleting the app stops any further diagnostics from being sent. See Sentry Privacy Policy

Usage analytics

To improve the app, statistics tied to a random number about what you do in it are sent to PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA):

  • Action names: app open/close (session length), which screen is shown and for how long, onboarding completed, a shot taken (which template, mode, filter, silent mode, number of decorations), photo/video saved, share, re-edit, gallery size, failures (kind and a short error message), Lifetime offer shown, purchase and restore results, and reaching the storage limit
  • App version, operating system, and a random identifier created when the app is installed (not linked to you; reinstalling creates a new one)

No photos, text you typed, name, email, advertising identifier, or location is sent. Your IP address is passed along in the connection. These statistics are used only to see which features are used and where people stop; they are not used to identify or track individuals.

  • Processed by: PostHog, Inc.
  • Transferred to: United States
  • Purpose: usage statistics to improve app features
  • Retention: up to 1 year
  • How to stop it: deleting the app stops any further statistics from being sent. See PostHog Privacy Policy

Third-party services

wemera uses these third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics, as described above.
  • PostHog — usage analytics, as described above.

wemera shows no ads. The Lifetime unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple; we receive no payment details.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and video.
  • Microphone — to record sound with your videos. It is not used for photos.
  • Media / Photos — to save your shots to your gallery, to load a photo you pick into a frame, and to show and delete saved photos in the app's gallery.
  • Motion (accelerometer) — to get the photo orientation right.

Children

wemera is not directed at children under 13.
