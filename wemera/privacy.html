wemera Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Your photos stay on your device

wemera is a camera app. All photos you capture are processed and stored only on your device. We do not upload, collect, or have any access to your photos. wemera has no server of its own and no user accounts.

What the app does not collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, location, or photos. There is no sign-up or login.

The one thing wemera does send off the device is crash and error diagnostics, described in the next section. It contains no personal information and no part of your photos.

Crash and error diagnostics

When the app crashes or a feature fails, we collect diagnostic information so we can find the cause and fix it. This includes the error and where it happened (stack trace), your device model, OS version, app version, and a record of the app starting and stopping.

It does not include your photos, anything you shot, text you typed, your name, email, phone number, location, or IP address. It is not used to identify you, and it is never used for advertising or marketing.

  • Processed by: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry)
  • Transferred to: United States
  • Purpose: diagnosing and fixing app errors
  • Retention: per Sentry's retention policy (90 days by default), then deleted
  • How to stop it: deleting the app stops any further diagnostics from being sent. See Sentry Privacy Policy

Usage analytics

To improve the app, anonymous statistics about what you do in it are sent to PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA):

  • Action names: app open/close (session length), which screen is shown and for how long, onboarding completed, a shot taken (which template, mode, filter, silent mode, number of decorations), photo/video saved, share, re-edit, gallery size, and failures (kind and a short error message)
  • App version, operating system, and a random identifier created when the app is installed (not linked to you; reinstalling creates a new one)

No photos, text you typed, name, email, advertising identifier, or location is sent, and IP addresses are discarded on receipt. These statistics are used only to see which features are used and where people stop; they are not used to identify or track individuals.

  • Processed by: PostHog, Inc.
  • Transferred to: United States
  • Purpose: usage statistics to improve app features
  • Retention: per PostHog's retention policy
  • How to stop it: deleting the app stops any further statistics from being sent. See PostHog Privacy Policy

Third-party services

wemera uses these third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics, as described above.
  • PostHog — usage analytics, as described above.

wemera shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and video.
  • Microphone — to record sound with your videos. It is not used for photos.
  • Media / Photos — to save your shots to your gallery, and to load a photo you pick into a frame.

Children

wemera is not directed at children under 13.

収集しないもの

氏名、メールアドレス、電話番号、連絡先、位置情報、写真は収集しません。登録・ログインは不要です。

エラー診断情報

写真、撮影した作品、入力した文字、氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号・位置情報・IPアドレスは含まれません。個人の特定には使用せず、広告やマーケティングにも使用しません。
