Privacy Policy

There is no sign-up. We don't ask for your name or contact details.

What we receive

  • IP address and request logs: used to stop too many requests. They leave server memory quickly, and stay in the server logs (Google Cloud) for up to 90 days.
  • Visit records: which space you were in and for how long, phone or PC, which app you came through, language, whether you've been here before, and the look number saved in your browser. Used only to improve the service. Deleted after up to 90 days.
  • Nickname: only if you set one. People in the same room can see it. The server holds it only while you're connected. It travels in the connection address, so it can end up in the logs.
  • Your character's position and actions in the space, and text sparks in friend rooms: passed to people in the same room and not stored.

What stays in your browser

Your nickname, look number, friend room code, and sound settings. Clearing site data removes them.

Who handles it

Server: Google Cloud (Seoul). Pages: Cloudflare (USA). Fonts: jsDelivr (USA). Your IP address reaches them over the internet when you connect. Retention is as above. If you don't want this, simply don't connect.

What we don't use

Ads, analytics tools, cookies, or your real-world location.
