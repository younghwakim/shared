Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

Buruda lets you reach the owner of a car by its licence plate, without anyone exchanging phone numbers. This policy explains what personal data Buruda handles, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

This English policy applies to people who use Buruda outside the Republic of Korea. People in Korea are covered by the Korean policy. A Traditional Chinese translation is provided for convenience; if the two differ, this English version prevails unless the law of your place of residence says otherwise.

1. Who we are

Buruda is operated by an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea (“we”, “us”). We decide how and why your data is used (the “data controller”), and the same person acts as the privacy / data protection officer.

Contact for anything about your data: younghwa.labs@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

3. Data we receive from other people

If you have registered a car, messages that other people send to your plate reach you through us. If someone reports or blocks you, we receive that report. This is data about you that comes from another person rather than from you.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account, your cars, the messages you received, reports about you and your consent records: until you delete your account. Deleting your account erases them immediately; it cannot be undone, and signing up again creates a new, empty account.
  • Messages you sent stay in the history of the person who received them, but are no longer linked to you once you delete your account. If someone had blocked you, the block stays in place for the device you sent from.
  • A car you remove: its plate, and the messages that car received, are erased when you remove it.
  • Backup copies of the database are kept for up to 7 days and then overwritten, so erased data disappears from backups within that time.
  • Phone numbers: we do not keep them. A note or a message that contains a phone number is not accepted, and the app tells you why. If one is written in a support request, it is removed before the text is stored.
  • Server logs (IP address, request time): up to 3 months.
  • Support requests you sent us: up to 3 years, as Korean consumer law requires records of complaints and disputes to be kept.
  • If a law requires us to keep something else longer, we keep only that, for only as long as required.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to stop using it, or to withdraw your consent. You can delete everything yourself in the app: Account → Delete account. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. You can also complain to the privacy authority where you live (see section 12).

8. Children

Buruda is not directed to children. Outside Korea, accounts are for people aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live, if higher). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that a minor has registered, we delete the account.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Plates and account identifiers are stored as keyed hashes and in encrypted form, so the database alone does not reveal them. Access to the server and the database is limited to the operator. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

10. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not use cookies for advertising. Because we do not track, we do not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app at least 30 days before the change takes effect. The current version and its effective date are always at the top of this page.
